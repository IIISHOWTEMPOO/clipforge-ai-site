Quick questions

Do you automatically generate clips with AI?

AI can help identify moments and create captions, but each delivered clip should be reviewed by a person. This starter website advertises a service; it does not process videos automatically.

What should I send you?

Send a video file or a link you have permission to share, along with the target platform, preferred style, and any sections to avoid.

Can you post the clips for me?

The starter packages cover editing and file delivery only. Posting, account management, and analytics can be quoted separately.

Can I use any video I find online?

No. Customers should own the footage or have permission to use it. Get permission before editing or republishing someone else’s content.
